ÞEL — a luxury label built on Icelandic materials

Fine inner-coat wool, salmon leather and an earth-dyed cloth nobody else makes, sold first to the two million visitors passing through Reykjavík, then online to the US and Europe. Own the material, not just the label.

Grace — a midlife wellness company for Europe

Six clean, physician-formulated supplements for women in perimenopause and menopause, bundled with a doctor-led eight-pillar lifestyle membership and bookable telehealth. The bottle is the entry; the doctor is the moat.
