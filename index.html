KAT — an autonomous futures and options trading bot

A Python bot on a hardened server that scans eight futures markets and options chains, takes day and swing positions through Interactive Brokers, and is meant to learn from its own wins and losses. Built and improved with Claude Code; currently trading a paper account. The business case is first wealth-building with own capital, and only later a signal or analysis service for other traders.

Dashboard

MarketOwn account first; retail signal buyers laterRetail traders pay €50–200 a month for signals, but the market is crowded and sceptical, and verified track records are rare.
Chance of success3 / 10Seven factors: founder fit 7, market 5, regulation 5, differentiation 3, economics 3, capital and time 3, key dependency 2. The dependency is an edge that has not yet shown up in paper trading; most retail algorithms never find one. The engineering and learning value is far higher than the money odds.What would raise it substantially: three months of paper trading with positive expectancy after fees, verified from the logs (+2); the same result on a small live account for three more months (+1); a tested circuit breaker that caps daily loss (+1); an order-flow data edge that shows up in the numbers, not in the story (+1).
Estimated cost€8 000 a year running · €40 000 with capitalServer, market data, signal subscriptions and broker fees of roughly €8 000 a year; plus €30 000 or more of trading capital at risk once it goes live, which must be treated as money that can be lost.

AFL — Icelandic strength nutrition

Iceland has produced more World's Strongest Man winners per head than any country, and the whole world knows it. AFL is a strength and recovery line built on what Icelanders ate and did to be strong for a thousand years: skyr, fish, seaweed, moss, cold water and lifting stones. The strongmen are the proof; the land is the product.

Products, each with a real Icelandic ingredient

  • Skyr protein powder — the hero product. Skyr is a 1,000-year-old strained high-protein dairy already selling in US supermarkets; blended with Icelandic grass-fed whey.
  • Cod protein and marine collagen — made in Iceland from cod skin, a zero-waste story that mirrors ÞEL's fish leather.
  • Icelandic moss (fjallagrös) recovery drink — the traditional remedy, positioned as the warm drink after the ice bath. Sold as tradition and ritual; the science is thin, so no health claims.
  • Electrolytes with Icelandic sea salt and kelp — hand-harvested geothermal salt plus seaweed minerals. Simple, cheap to make, high margin.
  • Omega-3 from Icelandic cod liver oil — sourced from the century-old Icelandic producer and branded as the recovery staple every Icelandic child grew up on.
  • Angelica and bilberry — the Viking-era medicinal plant, already extracted by an Icelandic company; a partnership, not research.
  • Lifting stones and programme — cast replicas of the Djúpalónssandur stones fishermen lifted to qualify for a boat, with a training plan. Costs almost nothing and makes the press.

Shares its EU manufacturer, lab testing and notifications with Grace, so the real cost of adding it as a second brand is lower than the figures suggest. Strongmen's names and likenesses and the "World's Strongest Man" name are licensed property: use the heritage, not the people, unless an ambassador signs.

MarketMen 25–50 in strongman, CrossFit and cold-plunge cultureSports nutrition is very large and crowded with cheap whey; the win is story and ingredients nobody else can claim, to buyers already spending €60–120 a month.
Chance of success4 / 10Seven factors: differentiation 7, market 6, founder fit 5, regulation 5, key dependency 4, economics 4, capital and time 3. Without a recognisable strongman or gym as partner it is a Viking-themed whey brand, of which there are many.What would raise it substantially: an ambassador or licensing deal with one recognisable Icelandic strongman or strongman gym (+2); skyr protein that wins a blind taste test against the market leaders (+1); 1,000 units pre-sold through strongman and cold-plunge communities (+1).

Six clean, physician-formulated supplements for women in perimenopause and menopause, bundled with a doctor-led eight-pillar lifestyle membership and bookable telehealth. The bottle is the entry; the doctor is the moat.
